How to read the numbers
+1.5
White is ahead by about one and a half pawns' worth. A minus sign (−1.5) means Black is ahead.
±0.3
Anything this close to zero is roughly equal. Around ±1 is a clear edge, ±3 or more is usually winning.
+M3
White has a forced checkmate in 3 moves (−M3: Black does).
★
Best move: the same move Stockfish chose.
?!
Inaccuracy: a better move existed, but the damage is small.
?
Mistake: the position got noticeably worse.
??
Blunder: a large swing, often losing material or the game. Mistakes and blunders are re-checked with a deeper search.
Accuracy
How close each player stayed to the engine's best play, from 0 to 100%.

Early in the game, small differences (under about 0.5) are often just engine noise at Quick or Standard depth. Choose Deep for steadier numbers.
